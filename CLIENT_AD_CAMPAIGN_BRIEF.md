# Your First Meta Ad Campaign — Plan

A quick summary of what we're setting up, so you know what's running and what to expect.

---

## What we're running

**One campaign, $10/day, promoting the app on both Facebook and Instagram.**

Because it's set up as an **app campaign**, Meta automatically sends each person to the right
place — iPhone users go to the **App Store**, Android users go to **Google Play**. You don't need
two separate campaigns.

**Instagram is included.** Using automatic placements, your ads appear on Instagram Feed, Stories,
Reels and Explore as well as Facebook — Meta puts your budget wherever it performs best that day.

---

## Who we're targeting

| Setting | What we chose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Location** | United States | Start focused; we can expand once we see what works |
| **Age** | 25 – 45 | The core age range for husbands and boyfriends in committed relationships |
| **Gender** | Men | The app is built specifically for him |
| **Interests** | Marriage, relationships, self-improvement, relationship authors (5 Love Languages, Mark Manson), plus "married"/"in a relationship" status | Signals someone in a relationship who's actively trying to improve |

**One important note on targeting:** at $10/day, **broader works better than narrow.** It feels
counter-intuitive, but if we over-filter the audience, Meta doesn't have enough room to find buyers
and the ads underperform. So we give Meta those interests as *hints* and let its algorithm do the
finding. As we spend more, we get more precise.

---

## The ads themselves

We've written **three different ads** to test three angles:
1. **"Know exactly what she needs"** — the practical, daily-guidance angle
2. **"She never sees it. She just feels it."** — the built-only-for-him angle
3. **"The 90-day partner challenge"** — the transformation angle

All three run at once, and Meta shifts budget toward whichever performs best. After a couple of
weeks we keep the winner and test new versions against it.

---

## What $10/day realistically gets you

$10/day is about **$300 for the month**. Honest expectations:

- Roughly **10,000–20,000** people see your ads
- Roughly **150–400** clicks
- Roughly **30–120 app installs**, likely costing **$3–$10 each**

That's a sensible testing budget. The goal for month one isn't profit — it's **learning which
message and audience work**, so that when you scale up, you're scaling something proven.

---

## What happens over the month

- **Week 1:** we leave everything alone. Changing things early resets Meta's learning and wastes
  money — this is the single most common and expensive mistake.
- **Week 2:** turn off the weakest ad, keep the budget steady.
- **Week 3:** add fresh creative to test against the winner.
- **Week 4:** full review — then decide whether to increase budget, change the audience, or try a
  new angle (which is exactly the "switch it up after a month" plan you described).

---

## What would help most from you

**Video.** For app ads, video consistently beats still images. Even something simple works:
a short screen recording of the app (the daily mission, a text being copied, the date idea), 15–30
seconds, with text on screen. If you can provide that, we'll get noticeably better results than
with images alone.

We also need:
- A **payment method** on the ad account
- Your **Instagram account** linked to the Facebook Page (so ads can run on Instagram)

---

## One thing to be aware of

Your **iPhone app** went to the App Store before we added Meta's measurement tool, so for now
**iOS install tracking will be incomplete** — the ads still run and still drive downloads, we just
can't attribute every iPhone install back to the specific ad. **Android tracking is fully in place.**
This is fixed with the next iOS app update, and doesn't stop us launching now.

Separately, Apple's privacy rules mean iPhone numbers are always partly estimated and delayed —
so we judge results over weeks, not days.
