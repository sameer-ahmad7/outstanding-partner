## What happens once you publish

- Meta reviews the ads first — usually under 24 hours.
- **Leave everything alone for the first week.** Changing budgets or targeting early resets Meta's
  learning and wastes money. This is the single most common and most expensive mistake.
- We review together after 7 days, switch off whatever isn't working, and put more behind what is.
- At roughly $10/day per campaign, expect in the region of **30–120 installs a month**. Month one is
  about learning which message works, not about profit.
