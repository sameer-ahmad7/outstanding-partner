# Google Play Setup — Client Guide

Outstanding Partner is already on the Apple App Store. This guide covers **your part** of
getting it onto **Google Play**: creating the Play account, creating the app, writing the store
description, and adding your developer as an admin. Once you add the developer, **they handle
everything technical** — the app file, screenshots, privacy forms, testing and submission.

**Your part takes ~30–45 minutes** (plus Google's identity verification, which can take a few days).

---

## Step 1 — Google Play Developer account (if you don't have one)

1. Go to **play.google.com/console** and sign in with the Google account you want to own the app.
2. Register as a developer:
   - Choose **Organization** (recommended for a business) or **Individual**.
   - Pay the **one-time $25 USD** registration fee.
3. Complete **identity verification** — Google asks for ID and/or business details.
   ⚠️ This can take **a few days** to approve, so start it early. Nothing else can publish until it clears.

> If you already have a Play Console account, skip to Step 2.

---

## Step 2 — Create the app

1. In Play Console, click **Create app** (top right).
2. Fill in:
   - **App name:** `Outstanding Partner`
   - **Default language:** English (United States)
   - **App or game:** App
   - **Free or paid:** **Free** (the subscription is an in-app purchase, so the app itself is Free)
3. Tick the **declarations** (Developer Program Policies + US export laws).
4. Click **Create app**.

> You do **not** type a "bundle" or "package name" here. The app's technical ID
> (`com.outstandingpartner.app`) is set automatically when your developer uploads the app file —
> it will match the iOS one. Nothing for you to enter.

---

## Step 3 — Write the store listing text

Go to **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing** and fill in the text fields. (Your developer
will add the screenshots and graphics — you only need the words.)

| Field | Limit | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **App name** | 30 characters | `Outstanding Partner` |
| **Short description** | 80 characters | One punchy line, e.g. *"Be the partner she brags about — daily missions, texts & date ideas."* |
| **Full description** | 4,000 characters | Reuse your **App Store description** — it works here almost as-is. Describe the app, the daily missions/texts/date ideas, the cycle-awareness, and the free trial. |

> **Tip:** paste in the same description you used on the App Store. Keep it honest and benefit-led;
> avoid superlatives like "best" or "#1", which Google can flag. Do **not** mention other app
> stores or pricing that contradicts the in-app price.

Save the draft — it doesn't publish until the whole app is submitted.

---

## Step 4 — Add your developer as an admin

This hands the technical work to your developer.

1. Play Console → **Users and permissions** (left sidebar, near the bottom).
2. Click **Invite new users**.
3. Enter the developer's email: **`sameer.ahmad3247@gmail.com`**
4. Under **Account permissions**, choose **Admin (all permissions)** — or at minimum grant this app
   with *Admin* app-level access.
5. Click **Invite user**. The developer accepts the email invite and takes over from here.

---

## What happens next (your developer handles all of this)

- Preparing and uploading the app file (signed release build)
- Screenshots, feature graphic, and app icon
- The **Data safety** form and **content rating** questionnaire
- Linking the subscription product and pricing
- Testing the app, then **submitting it for review**

Google's review of a first submission typically takes **a few days to ~1 week**. You'll get an
email when it's approved, and the app goes live on Google Play.
