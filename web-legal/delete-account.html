Delete Your Account

Outstanding Partner · Account & data deletion

This page explains how to delete your Outstanding Partner account and the data associated with it. You can do it yourself inside the app, or request deletion by email — both remove your data permanently from our systems.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Outstanding Partner app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete your account for you:

📧 Email a deletion request

Send an email to support@outstandingpartner.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request", from the email address on your account. We verify and process the request, and delete your data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove all of the following from our systems:

  • Your account and login credentials (email)
  • Your profile and your partner's details, favorites, and important dates
  • Cycle information you entered
  • Your notes ("She Said" journal), logs, progress, and settings
  • Your subscription records held by us

What may be retained

DataRetention
Your personal profile, partner data, cycle data, notes, logs, settingsDeleted permanently

Note: your subscription is billed through the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel an active store subscription — manage or cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

Delete some data without deleting your account

If you'd rather keep your account but clear your data, open the Profile tab and tap Reset All Data. This erases your partner's data, notes, logs, and settings while keeping your login.

Questions about your data? Email support@outstandingpartner.app and we'll help.
